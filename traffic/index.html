How It Works
Logic, methodology, and data sources behind this dashboard

Data Sources

Prodco — In-store people counter. Records every break in the sensor beam. Runs at all 5 stores. Raw data from STYLE_165 database.

PERQ — Sales floor CRM. Associates log every Up (first visit) and Be Back. Available Jan 2025 onward.

STYLE_165 Sales — ERP transaction data. Net sales = SUM(UNIT_PRICE × SND_QTY) including returns/negative lines. Dashboard uses net throughout.

Store Codes: 02=Cyrville · 03=Kingston · 04=Kanata · 05=Hunt Club · 06=Gatineau. Stores 01 (admin), 10 (online), 99 (HQ adjustments) excluded.

Traffic Conversion Model (TCM)

Step 1 — Employee Correction
Prodco records staff movement. Correction factors:
Cyrville 12% · Kanata 11% · Hunt Club 13% · Kingston 11% · Gatineau 10%
Net Visitors = Prodco × (1 − correction%)

Step 2 — Baseline Conversion Rate
Median (Ups+BB ÷ Net Visitors) per store × day-type (weekday/Sat/Sun) × month. Built from Jan 2025–present. Requires n ≥ 3 data points.

Step 3 — Expected Ups+BB
Expected = Net Visitors × Baseline Rate

Step 4 — Under-Reporting Index (URI)
URI = Reported ÷ Expected
✅ ≥ 90% = On target  |  ⚠️ 75–89% = Watch  |  🔴 < 75% = Gap

Traffic Goals

Ups Pace (current): from the 2026 Traffic Pacing sheet, Targets tab — per-store monthly Ups targets (Be Backs excluded), seasonal by month. All-store totals: Jan 2,871 · Feb 2,355 · Mar 2,883 · Apr 2,482 · May 2,649 · Jun 2,289 · Jul 2,481 · Aug 2,917 · Sep 2,741 · Oct 2,990 · Nov 3,580 · Dec 2,901 (annual 33,139). Drives the Monthly tab's Ups Pace and % to Ups Pace columns.

TV Lift Factor: TV-on weeks avg +12.3% traffic lift (2024–2025, 108 wks, p=0.0005). Pre-BF: +24.8%. Summer (Aug): +1.5% — negligible, supports dark period. Applied in the traffic pacing dashboard.

Sales Metrics Definitions

Net Sales: Includes returns/exchanges. Closest to PERQ-reported revenue.

$/Up: Net Sales ÷ (Ups+BB). Revenue per customer engaged (Ups and Be Backs).

$/Ticket: Net Sales ÷ Tickets. Average order value.

Conv%: Tickets ÷ (Ups+BB). % of engaged customers (Ups and Be Backs) who write an order.

YoY same-day: date − 364 days (52 × 7). Lands on same weekday, same season. E.g. Tuesday Jul 7, 2026 → Tuesday Jul 9, 2025.

Dashboard Refresh

This dashboard is built from a static snapshot of STYLE_165 data.

To refresh:
1. Run SQL data extraction (Prodco + PERQ + SALES_HISTORY)
2. Export compact_net.json
3. Run build3.py to regenerate HTML
4. Replace LZB Traffic Intelligence.html in the Team folder

A nightly scheduled task can automate this. Currently updated manually — data current as of the date shown in the header badge.
